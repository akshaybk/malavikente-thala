How to play

  1. Pick vs Computer or 1 v 1 from the menu. Player 1 is X, the opponent is O.
  2. Tap a square on the forehead. Three in a row — across, down or diagonally — wins the round.
  3. A match is 5 rounds; the next round starts automatically and the first move alternates each round.
  4. Most round wins takes the match, then the game resets back to the menu.
  5. Reset replays the current round; Main menu abandons the match.
