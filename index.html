How to play

  1. Two players, one screen. Player 1 is X, Player 2 is O — take turns tapping squares on the forehead.
  2. The status panel always shows whose turn it is. Starts flips who goes first in the next round.
  3. Get three in a row — across, down or diagonally — to win the round.
  4. Reset clears the board. Scores keep counting until you reload.
  5. No computer opponent — the forehead is just the battlefield.
